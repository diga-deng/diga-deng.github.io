02 / An open notebook

Questions worth keeping.

Not every exploration begins with an answer. These are the connections I want to keep investigating.

i.

When does a detail
become an identity?

A collar, a seam, a printed chain. Small choices can change how an otherwise familiar object feels.

A lens for looking

Compare the placement, scale and contrast of a detail. What remains recognizable when a logo disappears? Start with the identity and layering studies below.

ii.

Can AI expand a question,
not just supply an image?

I'm interested in the dialogue between human aesthetic judgment and AI-assisted creative exploration.

A working principle

Separate the reference, the prompt, the generated variation and the human decision. AI output should be labelled, and a visual concept should never be treated as proof that an object can be made or will work.

iii.

What happens after
the first owner?

Fashion also raises questions about use, fit, care and the next life of a garment.

A connection to practice

Clothing reuse brings aesthetics into contact with practical needs. Matching dimensions to a recipient's needs is a different problem from simply collecting more clothing.

03 / Behind the journal

Hello,
I'm Savannah.

Following curiosity across art, science, culture and technology.

ArtFashionAICulture

I'm drawn to art that feels alive: work that makes me notice a shape, reconsider a texture, or see something familiar differently.

This journal is a space for that curiosity. It brings together fashion references, observations about materials and silhouettes, and questions about the role of technology in creative expression.

The visual archive contains reference works by other designers and brands, with their sources clearly linked. These are studies and points of departure, not a claim of authorship, a shop, or a collection of finished original products.
