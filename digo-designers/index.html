Designer Recruitment

REMOTE COLLABORATION · COLLECTION-BASED DELIVERABLES · FEES NEGOTIABLE

DIGO is a Seattle-based team building an online shopping platform connecting independent brands, fashion, and home products. We provide fabric, garment development, and manufacturing partner resources, while managing production, operations, and promotion. Each Brand Lead Designer shapes the creative identity and product collections of a brand.

DIGO PROVIDES & MANAGES

Market research, customer insights, and project budgets.

Fabrics, trims, production techniques, cost information, and supply-chain resources.

Pattern making, sampling, garment production, and quality control.

AI videos, material explainers, and promotional films based on the designer's portfolio and style.

Platform operations, brand marketing, sales, logistics, and after-sales services.

BRAND LEAD DESIGNER OWNS

Proposing brand identity, collection themes, and product-category structure.

Using AI to rapidly develop, select, refine, and finalize coherent collections.

Overseeing silhouettes, construction, and materials; reviewing and revising samples remotely.

Providing creative concepts, references, and prompts; reviewing whether videos match the intended direction.

Leading future collections and proactively planning products based on feedback.

Remote Collaboration Workflow

01 / DESIGNER

Propose brand style and collection roadmap.

02 / DIGO

Provide market, fabric, cost, and production resources.

03 / DESIGNER

Develop, select, and refine collection designs with AI.

04 / DIGO

Arrange sampling, produce videos, and share feedback.

05 / DESIGNER

Review samples and videos; confirm the creative direction.

06 / DIGO

Settle fees as agreed, produce garments, and promote the collection.

DIGO produces the videos. Designers provide creative direction and feedback. All submissions and reviews take place remotely; no office attendance is required.

Lead a Brand's Collection Development

01

Distinctive Aesthetic & Creative Initiative

Demonstrate a clear personal style and complete collection work. Independently propose a brand vision and plan hero pieces, category combinations, silhouettes, construction, and future collections.

02

AI Collection Design Experience — Required

Show hands-on experience using AI to rapidly explore multiple styles, silhouettes, colors, and combinations. Maintain consistency across a collection, evaluate and revise outputs, and turn them into a cohesive series guided by real design judgment.

03

Fabric Knowledge & Finished-Product Experience

Understand how materials, patterns, construction, and craftsmanship interact. Demonstrate experience with premium garments or high-quality original collections, including samples, finished pieces, or realized projects, with your individual contributions identified.

Key deliverables: collection planning, AI design iterations, final styles and detail specifications, sample reviews, and creative-direction feedback on promotional content.
